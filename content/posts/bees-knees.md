The Bees Knees dates back to Prohibition-era America, when honey and lemon were used to mask the harsh taste of bathtub gin. Turns out, the combination is so good it outlasted the era that created it. With quality gin, it's one of the most elegant three-ingredient cocktails you can make.

## Bees Knees

- 60 milliliters (2 ounces) London dry gin
- 22 milliliters (¾ ounce) fresh lemon juice
- 22 milliliters (¾ ounce) honey syrup (recipe below)
- Lemon twist, for garnish

The key to a great Bees Knees is balance. The honey rounds out the citrus without making it cloying, and the gin's botanicals tie everything together. Use a London dry gin for a classic profile, or experiment with floral gins to lean into the honey notes.

1. Combine the gin, lemon juice, and honey syrup in a cocktail shaker.
2. Fill with ice and shake vigorously for 12-15 seconds until well chilled.
3. Double strain into a chilled coupe or Nick and Nora glass.
4. Express a lemon twist over the surface, then drop it in or perch it on the rim.

## Honey Syrup

- 100 grams honey
- 100 milliliters hot water

Honey is too viscous to mix directly into a cold cocktail, so you need to thin it into a syrup first. A 1:1 ratio by weight keeps plenty of honey flavor while making it easy to pour and measure.

1. Combine the honey and hot water in a jar.
2. Stir until fully dissolved.
3. Cool and refrigerate. Keeps for 2-3 weeks.
